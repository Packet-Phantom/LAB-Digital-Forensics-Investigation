# LAB: Digital Forensics Investigation

<h2>Description</h2>
  <b>This lab demonstrates the process of conducting a digital forensic investigation of an employee's work laptop, on behalf of a company that suspects the individual is an internal threat actor (But in a controlled lab environment). The exercise focuses on creating a forensic case, examining digital evidence, recovering deleted artifacts, analyzing metadata, and constructing a timeline of events to determine whether unauthorized activity occurred. The investigation culminates in the identification, analysis, and documentation of evidence to support findings and conclusions regarding potential policy violations and data exfiltration activities from the internal employee (named John Smith).</b>

  This project was completed using a preconfigured virtual machine (VM) containing the required evidence files and lab environment. Because the VM was prebuilt for instructional purposes, the project cannot be replicated exactly as documented. However, the lab still provides valuable insight into the digital forensics investigation process and demonstrates the methodologies used to collect, analyze, and interpret digital evidence in a real-world investigation scenario.
